Scope: src/app/(marketing)/page.tsx + components/home/Hero.tsx. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: private-university students, phone-first, evening/commute light. Action: Join PUNAB (/register). Proof: real event albums, live DB counts (chapters, events, albums). Constraints: logo + crimson stay; CMS keys and data wiring stay; Bangla + English; low bandwidth (no heavy imagery, CSS/SVG texture only).
