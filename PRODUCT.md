# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Private-university students/members: sign up, join chapters, register for events (IMUN, BABBF championship, Futsal, July Award), use member dashboard.
- Public visitors/prospects: browse about, leadership, notices, archive, join.
- BloodHero donors and requesters: urgent blood requests, donor matching.
- Admins/CMS staff: role-gated main CMS and BloodHero admin.

## Product Purpose
PUNAB (Private University National Association of Bangladesh) web platform: public site, membership, event registration, awards, certificates, and BloodHero blood-donation coordination. Success = members join and register smoothly, donors respond to urgent requests, admins run content and events without developer help.

## Positioning
National network uniting private universities across Bangladesh AND a student-run civic platform: chapters, events, awards, and BloodHero under one roof. Neighbors (single-university clubs, generic blood apps) cannot claim both.

## Operating Context
Live at www.punab.com. Zones: (marketing) public, (bloodhero) donor/request flows, (bloodhero-admin), admin/ CMS, dashboard/ member area. Event campaigns run seasonally (Eid cards, July Award 2026).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind 4, Supabase Auth/Storage, Prisma, Resend.
- Bilingual English + Bangla: Bangla text must render well.
- Mobile-first, low-bandwidth audiences.

## Brand Commitments
Keep existing PUNAB name and logo (assets in public/) — binding.

## Product Principles
- Serve both roles: national association and civic service.
- Mobile-first, light payloads.
- Bangla and English equal citizens.
- Urgent flows (BloodHero) beat decoration.
- Admins self-serve.
